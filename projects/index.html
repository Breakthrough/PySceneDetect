Projects & Research

This page lists open-source projects, research papers, and similar tools related to PySceneDetect. To suggest an addition, open an issue on GitHub.

Projects Using PySceneDetect

The open-source projects below use PySceneDetect in their own code. They are listed for reference only: PySceneDetect does not maintain or endorse them, and each project has its own license.

Datasets and model training

  • LTX-2: cuts training videos into scenes with split_scenes.py.
  • Data-Juicer: splits videos at scene changes with its video_split_by_scene_mapper operator.
  • OneTrainer: finds cuts with AdaptiveDetector when extracting training clips.
  • VideoX-Fun: detects cuts in its video captioning pipeline.

Video generation

  • ViMax: splits generated transition videos into shots with split_video_ffmpeg.
  • InfiniteTalk: detects shots in input videos by combining the content, adaptive, and threshold detectors.

Agents, RAG, and data tools

  • ECC: measures the cut cadence of a reference video by sweeping ContentDetector thresholds.
  • RAG-Anything: splits videos into scenes before indexing them.
  • CAMEL: extracts keyframes at scene changes in VideoAnalysisToolkit.
  • Skill Seekers: finds scene boundaries in scraped videos before extracting keyframes.
  • OmAgent: splits videos into scenes for its video understanding agent.
  • Pixeltable: provides scene_detect video functions with an example notebook.
  • MassGen: extracts frames from each scene in its video extraction tool.

Applications and tools

  • Moondream: detects scenes before running its content moderation model.
  • REAL Video Enhancer: includes ContentDetector to catch scene changes during rendering.
  • movie-narrator: detects scenes as part of its movie recap pipeline.

Research and Literature

The papers below either use PySceneDetect for video analysis or compare new shot-boundary detection methods against it:

  • Panda-70M: Captioning 70M Videos with Multiple Cross-Modality Teachers by Tsai-Shien Chen, Aliaksandr Siarohin, Willi Menapace, Ekaterina Deyneka, Hsiang-wei Chao, Byung Eun Jeon, Yuwei Fang, Hsin-Ying Lee, Jian Ren, Ming-Hsuan Yang, and Sergey Tulyakov (2024).
  • Stable Remaster: Bridging the Gap Between Old Content and New Displays by Nathan Paull, Shuvam Keshari, and Yian Wong (2023).
  • LoL-V2T: Large-Scale Esports Video Description Dataset by Tsunehiko Tanaka and Edgar Simo-Serra (2021).
  • Online Detection of Action Start via Soft Computing for Smart City by Tian Wang, Yang Chen, Hongqiang Lv, Jing Teng, Hichem Snoussi, and Fei Tao (2020).
  • Thesis Project: Smart Shades and Cane for The Blind by Muhammad Hashim (2020).
  • Movienet: a movie multilayer network model using visual and textual semantic cues by Youssef Mourchid, Benjamin Renoust, Olivier Roupin, Lê Văn, Hocine Cherifi, and Mohammed El Hassouni (2019).
  • NLP-Enriched Automatic Video Segmentation by Mohannad AlMousa, Rachid Benlamri, and Richard Khoury (2018).
  • Online Detection of Action Start in Untrimmed, Streaming Videos by Zheng Shou, Junting Pan, Jonathan Chan, Kazuyuki Miyazawa, Hassan Mansour, Anthony Vetro, Xavi Gir-i-Nieto, and Shih-Fu Chang (2018).
  • Story Understanding in Video Advertisements by Keren Ye, Kyle Buettner, and Adriana Kovashka (2018).

Publicly accessible links are preferred when suggesting new citations, though not required.

Scene detection methodology

You can inspect the implementation of every built-in detector in scenedetect/detectors/. For background discussion on references and methods, see Issue #62.

Similar Programs

These tools and models also detect scene cuts in video:

  • Scenecut Extractor: wraps the ffmpeg select filter.
  • ffmpeg blackframe filter (thanks @tonycpsu): detects fades to black only.
  • Shotdetect: content-based detector for Linux and macOS.
  • MATLAB Scene Change Detection: uses feature extraction and edge detection (requires MATLAB and Computer Vision Toolbox).
  • chaptertool: CLI and web tool that converts PySceneDetect output to other formats.
  • TransNetV2: neural network for shot boundary detection (2020).
  • AutoShot: neural network for shot boundary detection using neural architecture search (2023).
