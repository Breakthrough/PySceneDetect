## Common Use Cases

People use PySceneDetect for many video workflows:

- Splitting home videos or raw footage into separate scene clips.
- Finding and removing commercials from recorded TV broadcasts.
- Reviewing and splitting security camera recordings.
- Finding clean loops in videos for GIFs and cinemagraphs.
- Analyzing film and video datasets, such as measuring average shot length.

[Download PySceneDetect](download.md) to try it on your own videos, or review the [Features](features.md) page for a full list of detection methods and export formats. The default timecode format (`HH:MM:SS.nnn`) works directly with tools such as `ffmpeg` and `mkvmerge`.
